# map.md — src/ (enclave service)

The entire attested Kioku backend: it terminates TLS and serves OAuth, sync, MCP/REST,
account, quotas, capture-session feedback, APNs ready receipts, and the summarizer—see [`cp/`](cp/map.md)—alongside authenticated
`410 Gone` tombstones for the retired `/v1/*` query/storage API. Plaintext databases exist only here and in SEV tmpfs, never on
persistent disk; bounded audio, screenshot pixels, transcript/screen text, and metadata
leave the TEE through the documented Vertex inference boundary, while explicitly
configured webhook events use the separate webhook boundary.

The ADR-0022 Phase-1 advisory owner is **deleted**, not dormant. `#289` (`9b2f87e`)
removed `archive_v3_advisory_owner.rs` and its whole 13-module directory — the canary
capability and three-root `canary_trust.rs` verifier, the owner lifecycle, controller,
window observer, telemetry, comparison, and Phase-2 acquisition — together with the
Phase-2 admission and its compile-pinned `full_reviewed_mutation_set_commitment`. The
genesis-first replan drops retention of existing archive data, so that migration-and-observe
route to authority was removed rather than finished; an archive is now created
WAL-authoritative from genesis (`archive_v3_genesis.rs`) and its schema evolves through the
append-only ladder (`schema_ladder.rs`). Dropping the pinned mutation-set commitment is what
let `#290` add `WalOperationKind::SchemaEpochAdvance = 13` with no offline signing ceremony.

What survives is plumbing, not a path: the eight advisory/Phase-2 control-store tables keep
their DDL and migration (removal is a separate atomic PR — see [`cp/map.md`](cp/map.md)) but
have no writer outside tests, and the witness's advisory-terminal and Phase-2 succession
predicates still compile with intra-module callers that nothing outside the
witness/Firestore pair reaches. `Phase2AcquisitionStage` kept `from_db` and lost `as_db`:
old rows decode, new ones cannot be minted.
