# Single-archive WAL owner child map

| Path | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `launcher.rs` | Private inactive composition owner. It consumes exactly one non-cloneable serving handoff — the parity-certified completed maintenance handoff or the genesis-ledger handoff carrying the durable genesis owner reservation — owns one heterogeneous sealed-plan actor for the archive, and never exposes or clones the actor handle. There is no caller, startup/config/route/Store-registry/acknowledgement wiring, provider list/delete authority, deployment, or cloud mutation. |
| `publisher.rs` | Private inactive publisher/checkpoint implementation. It is called only by the private sibling launcher after one serving handoff is consumed: the maintenance lane reauthenticates the exact terminal Control row against the carried parity evidence, while the genesis lane re-adopts the durable owner reservation off the genesis control ledger (whose reservation path revalidates the ledger row's exact terminal bytes) and requires exact equality with the carried reservation — the two lane authorities are mutually exclusive by guard, and everything downstream is one shared ladder. It durably owns the exact witness lease, stages logical WAL objects through Control, and performs bounded two-pass checkpoint source retirement, reserve/create/readback recovery, exact witness advance, and fresh recovered-owner settlement. It exposes no external domain adapter, route, Store factory, acknowledgement, list/delete operation, or startup/config/runtime activation. |
